## Getting Started

### Registration
To start using the platform, you need to register an account.
1. Go to the registration page.
2. Enter your personal details (Name, Email, Password).
3. Provide your **Company Information** (Name, Description, Website).
   > **Tip:** Be descriptive about your company! Our AI uses your company description to personalize your experience and match you with relevant opportunities.

### Dashboard
Once logged in, you will see your personalized dashboard. This is your command center for all procurement activities.

## For Buyers

As a Buyer, your main goal is to create Requests for Quotation (RFQs) and find the best suppliers.

### Creating an RFQ
1. Navigate to the **"Create RFQ"** section.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Title**: Clear and concise title (e.g., "Office Furniture for New Branch").
   - **Description**: Detailed specifications of what you need.
   - **Budget**: Your price range (optional).
   - **Deadline**: When you need the bids by.
3. Click **"Save Draft"** to review or **"Publish"** to make it live immediately.

### Managing RFQs
- **Drafts**: RFQs that are not yet visible to suppliers. You can edit them freely.
- **Open**: Live RFQs collecting bids. You cannot edit major details once open to ensure fairness.
- **Closed**: RFQs that are no longer accepting bids.

### Selecting a Winner
1. Go to your Open RFQ.
2. View the list of submitted Bids.
3. Use the **AI Summary** to quickly compare proposals.
4. Click **"Select Winner"** on the best bid to award the contract.

## For Suppliers

As a Supplier, your goal is to find relevant RFQs and submit winning bids.

### Finding Opportunities
1. Go to the **"Marketplace"** or **"Open RFQs"** section.
2. Browse the list of active requests.
3. Filter by industry or requirements to find matches for your capabilities.

### Submitting a Bid
1. Open an RFQ that interests you.
2. Click **"Submit Bid"**.
3. Enter your proposal:
   - **Price**: Your competitive offer.
   - **Delivery Time**: How quickly you can fulfill the order.
   - **Message**: A personalized note to the buyer.
4. Click **"Submit"**. You can update your bid anytime before the deadline.

## AI Features

Our platform leverages advanced AI to streamline your workflow.

### AI Negotiation Assistant
*Available for both Buyers and Suppliers.*
Struggling to find the right words? Use our AI to draft professional negotiation messages.
- **How to use**: When viewing a bid, click **"Negotiate"**. Enter your goal (e.g., "Ask for a 5% discount"), and the AI will generate a polite and effective message for you.

### AI Co-pilot Chat
Need advice? The AI Co-pilot is always ready to help.
- **Ask about RFQs**: "Is this budget realistic for these requirements?"
- **Ask about Bids**: "What are the risks in this proposal?"
- **General Advice**: "How can I improve my supplier rating?"
